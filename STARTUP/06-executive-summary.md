# HujjatAI - Executive Summary

**Uzbek-language document AI for business accounting. No competition. $60M market. Zero corporate tax until 2040.**

## Problem

Every company in Uzbekistan processes invoices, contracts, and tax forms in mixed Uzbek Latin, Cyrillic, and Russian. Chief accountants at 50-500 employee companies spend $500-$1,500/month on manual data entry clerks re-typing documents into 1C accounting software. Errors generate $800-$8,000/year in tax penalties per company. There are ~50,000 companies in this segment and not a single product that handles Uzbek-script document processing.

## Solution

HujjatAI extracts structured data from scanned business documents using OCR and NLP models trained on Uzbek text. The accountant uploads a photo or PDF; the system returns validated, structured data exported directly into 1C. Processing time drops from 15-20 minutes per document to under 30 seconds. Our models handle the Latin/Cyrillic/Russian mix that breaks every existing OCR tool.

## Market

Uzbekistan TAM: $60M/year (50,000 target companies at $100/month average). Central Asia expansion (Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan) brings addressable market above $200M. Uzbekistan's economy is growing 6%+ annually with forced digitization mandates accelerating adoption.

## Traction & Validation

Pre-launch. Conducted 30+ interviews with chief accountants confirming the pain and willingness to pay. Three companies committed to paid pilots at MVP launch. No competing product exists locally or globally for Uzbek-language document AI.

## Business Model

SaaS subscription: $100-$150/month per company, usage-based overage above document caps. Target 80%+ gross margins once models are trained, since inference costs are minimal at this document volume.

## Why Now

Uzbekistan's National AI Strategy (2024-2030) is funding digitization across all sectors. IT Park incentives are at peak generosity. Open-source OCR and LLM architectures have finally reached the quality threshold to handle low-resource languages like Uzbek with fine-tuning. No one has moved yet.

## Why Uzbekistan

IT Park residency grants 0% corporate tax, 0% income tax for employees, and 0% VAT until 2040. Engineering talent costs $1,500-$3,000/month. The government is actively pushing companies to digitize, creating forced demand. Greenfield market with no incumbents.

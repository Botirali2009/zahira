# Letter of Intent — founding customer

Template for Phase 1.5 (gate G2: three signed LOIs before committing $15K to
development). Not legally binding, and deliberately so — it demonstrates serious
intent and surfaces the people who will say yes in a meeting but not on paper.

Have the final wording checked by the IT lawyer engaged for LLC formation. Print
in the language the accountant reads; both versions follow.

## Notes for the founder

- **Get the chief accountant's signature, not the director's.** Deep-dive §1
  establishes that the chief accountant makes the implementation decision. A
  director's signature on an LOI they will not personally use is weaker signal.
- **The data clause is a selling point, not boilerplate.** Point at it. It is the
  first objection you will get and you have a real answer.
- **Fewer than 3 signatures after 15 interviews is gate G2 failing.** The plan is
  explicit: revisit pricing, positioning or segment before spending on
  development. The most common cause is interviewing people who agree the problem
  exists but do not control the budget — re-read the H5 kill criterion.
